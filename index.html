FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are the answers to common questions about the course, preparations, and Sudarshan Kriya.

What is Sudarshan Kriya®?

Sudarshan Kriya® is a powerful, rhythmic breathing technique developed by Gurudev Sri Sri Ravi Shankar. It incorporates natural rhythms of breath to release deep-seated physical and mental stress, harmonize body systems, and clear negative emotions, leaving you calm, focused, and energized.

Who can participate in this program?

The Happiness Program is open to anyone aged 18 and above. It is equally suited for complete beginners as well as experienced practitioners of yoga and meditation. No prior training or flexibility is required!

Do I need to attend all three days?

Yes. The Happiness Program is a progressive, experiential workshop. The techniques, including Sudarshan Kriya, are taught step-by-step. Attending all three sessions is essential to complete the training, learn the home practice safely, and receive the maximum benefits.

What should I wear and bring?

Please wear loose, comfortable clothing suitable for light stretches and sitting on the floor. Bring your own yoga mat (if you have one), a bottle of drinking water, and a pen. Chairs are fully available at the Gurukul center for anyone who has back issues or difficulties sitting on the floor.

Is there parking available at the center?

Yes, there is street parking available for two-wheelers and limited street parking for four-wheelers around Gurukul (Sector 2 park area). We recommend arriving 10-15 minutes early on the first day to settle in comfortably.
